## Summary

Opal Camera, the San Francisco startup known for high-end webcams, is rebranding as Opal Electronics and moving into a broader range of consumer hardware. The company is positioning itself less as a webcam maker and more as a design-led electronics brand with AI-focused products.

The shift is backed by a $40 million Series B round led by OpenAI, with additional investors reported to include Samsung, Peter Thiel, Alexis Ohanian's Seven Seven Six, and Marques Brownlee. The round values Opal at roughly $275 million.

Opal is working on an AI-powered audio device expected to launch within a few months of the article. The product is being tested by people connected to OpenAI, xAI, Anthropic, and Thinking Machines. The report says it is in a familiar product category and is not meant to compete directly with the iPhone.

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman was reportedly an early fan of Opal's C1 webcam. A prior meeting between OpenAI and Opal explored whether OpenAI's Whisper transcription model could run locally on Opal cameras for live captions, and the Opal team later saw an early preview of ChatGPT.

Although OpenAI is now Opal's largest shareholder, the article reports that OpenAI does not control Opal's IP or product designs. Opal can partner with whichever AI labs it chooses, and its upcoming audio product is expected to support a specific AI lab partnership while keeping other model relationships open.

Opal says it plans to stay small and release documentation, manufacturing plans, circuit board schematics, and software to the public when its products reach end of life, so owners can keep devices useful after official support ends.

The piece frames Opal's approach as more cautious than earlier AI hardware efforts such as Humane's Ai Pin and Rabbit's R1, both of which struggled after launch. Opal appears to be trying to avoid overpromising while building toward a more durable consumer electronics portfolio.

## Key Details

- Opal Camera is becoming Opal Electronics.
- The company raised $40 million in Series B funding led by OpenAI.
- Reported valuation: about $275 million.
- The first new product is an AI-powered audio gadget.
- The launch window reported in the article was three to four months from June 2, 2026.
- Opal plans two more products within the following year.
- OpenAI is the largest shareholder but reportedly does not own Opal's designs or IP.
- Opal has sold more than 50,000 webcams and manufactures in Taiwan.
